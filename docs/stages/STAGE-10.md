# Stage 10 record: Phase C, quotations and invoices

Source: `docs/ADDITIONAL-REQUIREMENTS.md` (Phase C, owner decisions 5 and 6), `docs/adr/0006-commercial-documents.md`, booklet ch. 13.6 (quotes), 13.7 and 27.6 (payments, ATOL timing), 14.9 (documents), 17 (notifications), 35.1 rules 3, 4 and 10. Measured locally on 24 Oct 2026. The invoice object and the same-day rule are not in the booklet, hence ADR-0006.

## What changed (DOCUMENTED)
- ADR-0006 (C1-C12): the quotation is the sent quote plus a number, PDF and email; the invoice is a new aggregate; expiry is `CANCELLED` with reason `EXPIRED` (no extra state); the PAY_ON_TICKETING guard and the Europe/London day; numbering; particulars; delivery; visibility; the payment seam; the expiry job.
- Contracts (`packages/contracts/src/invoices.ts`): `InvoiceStatus`, `CustomerInvoice` (no draft representable), `CustomerQuotation` (no amount), `BillingDocuments`, `StaffInvoice` (amounts nullable without `pricing.read.internal`), `InvoiceCancelBody`, `CommercialDocumentSettings`; `AppSettings.commercialDocuments` (prefixes, validity days, registration number, VAT number, VAT treatment, address; all particulars default to `null` = UNKNOWN); notification events `quotation.sent`, `invoice.issued`, `invoice.cancelled`, `invoice.expired` and their default matrix rules (cancelled and expired are critical: "do not pay this" must not wait for quiet hours).
- Domain (`packages/domain/src/invoice.ts`, framework-free): `INVOICE_TRANSITIONS` (issue, cancel, expire, mark_paid, mark_refunded), `londonDay`, `endOfLondonDay` (DST aware), `invoiceExpiry`, `formatDocumentNumber`, `invoiceAmountsEditable`.
- Database, migration `0006_commercial_documents` (hand-written, journal entry by hand): `document_sequences`, `invoices`, `invoice_lines`, `invoice_status_history` (insert-only: trigger plus `IMMUTABLE_TABLES`), `quotes.document_number` (unique) and `quotes.pdf_document_id`; triggers freeze `total_minor`, `currency`, `number`, `quote_id` and the lines once an invoice leaves DRAFT; a partial unique index allows one live (DRAFT, ISSUED, PAID) invoice per quote; CHECK that every non-draft invoice has a number and expiry. Seed: en/fa INBOX, PUSH, SMS and EMAIL templates for the four events (32 rows, `db:seed` re-run on the existing database).
- API: `DocumentNumbersService` (gap-free, concurrency-safe: counter row incremented inside the issuing transaction), `InvoicesService` (draft from the accepted quote, issue, cancel, expire, `markPaid`/`markRefunded`/`settleFromPayments` as the Phase D seam, views), `CustomerBillingController` (`GET /v1/billing/documents`, `GET /v1/billing/invoices/:number`; own only, 404 otherwise), `AdminInvoicesController` (list, detail, per request, create draft, issue, cancel). Quote send now allocates the quotation number in the same transaction (flight and cargo, including the re-weigh revised quote) and carries it in the outbox event; the flight quote PDF is rebuilt with the number and company particulars, and a cargo quotation PDF is new. Offline payment recording and four-eyes verification call `settleFromPayments`. Delivery: `NotifyInput.documentDelivery` forces inbox and email whatever the stored matrix says and falls back to SMS when no email is verified; the router sends `quotation.sent` (replacing the generic `quote.sent` notice to the customer; the timers still listen to `quote.sent`) and the three invoice events. Expiry: per-invoice delayed job `inv-<id>--invoice.expire` through `TimersService` (rebuilt by its reconcile) plus a `maintenance` repeatable `invoices.expire-due` every 5 minutes. Priced PDFs (`QUOTE_PDF`, `INVOICE`) open for staff only with `pricing.read.internal`.
- PDF: `renderPdf` gained a brand header (Night flight navy and orange), a kind label and a table section; `commercial-pdf.ts` prints "UNKNOWN - to be confirmed by the accountant" for every unset particular.
- Permissions: none new (`commercial_document.issue`, `pricing.read.internal`, `pricing.write` from migration 0003). Create draft, issue, cancel: `commercial_document.issue` (admin, owner). Reads: `flight_request.read.any`, `cargo_request.read.any` or `commercial_document.issue`, each limited to its product line unless the caller can issue. No grant migration, no `perm_version` bump, no Valkey clear needed.
- i18n: `billing.*` and `nav.billing` (en/fa), pinned by `packages/i18n/src/billing.spec.ts`; Flutter: 32 ARB entries through `tool/gen_arb.py`.
- Web (`/en`, `/fa`): "Documents & billing" in the signed-in navigation and the footer, `/billing` (invoices with amounts and status, quotations without amounts, PDF download) and `/billing/[number]` (the target of the email and SMS short link; sign-in first). Components `invoice-status.tsx`; no existing screen restyled.
- Flutter: `BillingScreen`, `InvoiceScreen` (routes `/account/billing`, `/account/billing/:number`, the web path `/billing/<number>` redirects there), a link on the Account tab.
- Console: `InvoicesPanel` on the flight and cargo request pages (create draft, Issue with the server's guard reason shown next to the disabled button, Cancel with reason and internal note, status history) and an Admin Panel `/invoices` list with a status filter.
- Console test flake fixed: seeding through the API no longer signs staff in again. `apiSessionFrom(page, ADMIN)` builds the API session from the signed-in page's cookies (the second factor is re-confirmed, no OTP request); `receivedShipment` accepts a session. The mobile triage test signs in the admin (whose desktop sign-in was minutes earlier) instead of the owner. No rate limit was changed.
- OpenAPI regenerated (156 paths, was 149). DPIA: invoice data category added; email vendor unchanged.

## Limits and open items
- UNKNOWN: company registration number, VAT number, VAT treatment and registered address (decision 5, accountant); the PDFs show the placeholder and no VAT lines. The numbering format is configurable (`commercialDocuments` in the app settings, edited in the Developer Panel settings) but the accountant's preferred format is not known; the default is `INV-YYYY-NNNNNN` / `QT-YYYY-NNNNNN`.
- INFERRED: "same-day ticketing" means the tickets were recorded on the same Europe/London calendar day as the invoice is issued; tickets from an earlier day block the issue permanently (a staff member cannot override it). Counsel or the accountant should confirm, and say whether such a request needs another path.
- INFERRED: under PAY_ON_TICKETING the money is taken with the tickets, so a flight invoice issued afterwards is usually issued as PAID (it settles from the verified payment record in the same transaction, history `issue` then `mark_paid`).
- The customer's address is printed when present but not required: decision 9 is PROPOSED, so an invoice does not wait for it.
- Invoice PDFs are English only and Latin-only (the standard PDF font drops Persian characters), as for the other documents.
- Online payment is not built (Phase D): the invoice page says payment instructions come from the Uni Travel contact. Refunds are not recorded by any current flow, so `mark_refunded` has no caller yet.
- Cargo request pages open for `cargo_admin` and `owner`, invoices are issued by `admin` and `owner`: in the console only the owner can therefore create, issue and cancel a cargo invoice (the admin can list it and use the API). Flight invoices are fully workable by the admin. Whether `admin` should see cargo requests is an owner decision, not changed here.
- BLOCKED: Flutter web build and smoke test not rerun (`flutter build web` and the shared :4300 serving are the brand session's).
