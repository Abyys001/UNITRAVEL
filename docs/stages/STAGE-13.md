## What changed (DOCUMENTED)
- Contracts: `TravellerPatch` (no defaults); `PriceLine`, `PriceBreakdown` with optional `extras` (max 10, each > 0) and `discount`; `AmendFlightRequestBody`; timeline kind `AMENDMENT`; error `COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` (409); events `request.amended`, `invoice.paid`, `payment.requested`, `payment.failed`, `payment.refunded` with default matrix rules.
- Domain (`packages/domain/src/amendments.ts`, framework-free): `amendState` (NEW, IN_REVIEW, AWAITING_CUSTOMER, QUOTED, ACCEPTED; acknowledgement while a quote is out), `priceBreakdownTotal`, `priceBreakdownIssue` (currency, negatives, discount above the price, total not positive, total not equal to the computed sum). Used by the API and by the console preview.
- Database, migration `0009_request_amendments` (hand-written, journal entry by hand): insert-only `request_amendments` (trigger plus `IMMUTABLE_TABLES`). Seed: 40 template rows (5 events × en/fa × inbox, push, SMS, email).
- API: quote validation uses the domain total; invoice drafts add one line per extra and a negative `Discount - <label>` line; the quotation PDF prints them. `POST /v1/admin/flight-requests/:ref/amend` (`flight_request.transition`): version check, reason, passenger mix, own travellers only, new travellers screened and the new route checked against route policy (a hit refuses the change and opens a case), SSRs of removed travellers dropped, before/after row, audit `flight_request.amended` (or `flight_request.amend_refused`), customer notification. Staff detail carries `amendment`; staff timeline shows previous and new values, customer timeline only "Details updated by Uni Travel". Payment events: `InvoicesService.markPaid` emits `invoice.paid` (card and verified offline alike), a failed asynchronous card payment emits `payment.failed`, each increase of the refunded amount emits `payment.refunded`, the resend action emits `payment.requested`. All are sent as documents (inbox and email; SMS when no verified email) with no amounts. OpenAPI regenerated (180 paths, was 179).
- Console: quote builder rows for extra services and a discount, total previewed with the domain function; "Amend trip" dialog on the request page (route, dates, flexibility, cabin, reason; acknowledgement checkbox while a quote is out). Travellers can be changed through the API; the dialog leaves them as they are.
- Web: the customer's quote shows extras and the discount (`itinerary.discount`, en and fa).
- Flutter: no change needed (it shows the quote total and keeps timeline kinds as strings).
- Permissions: none new; no `perm_version` bump.
