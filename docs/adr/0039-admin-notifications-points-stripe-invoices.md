# ADR-0039 · Admin dashboard: staff notifications, club points, Stripe invoices, section guidance

Status: accepted. Requested by the owner on 21 Nov 2026 ("Task 1: Improve and Complete the Admin Dashboard"); plan approved the same day. Owner decisions: club points as a staff-managed ledger with no earning rule; the "APY Usage" (Apify usage) card removed from every panel; Stripe test keys entered by the owner in the Developer Panel. Stage record: `docs/stages/STAGE-30.md`.

Amends ADR-0028 D7 (no Stripe Invoicing), ADR-0016 (club has no points) and ADR-0032 B3 (Apify usage card on both homes). ADR-0007 (test mode only, live BLOCKED), ADR-0006 (our numbering and frozen amounts) and rule 5 (online payment governed by ONLINE_PAYMENT policies) stay in force.

## Context
- Staff got inbox rows only when on duty, only for new requests and service requests. A new support ticket notified nobody, and the console never read the inbox. The console listened for `support-changed`, but the API never sent it. (OBSERVED)
- The sidebar already drew `count` badges, but they showed queue sizes rather than unread items. (OBSERVED)
- "Add member" was disabled while no active tier existed, and the seed creates no tier, so a fresh club was a dead end. (OBSERVED)
- The club ledger holds money credit; points did not exist. (OBSERVED)
- Payment was Embedded Checkout only. There was no Stripe Customer mapping and no Stripe Invoice. (OBSERVED)
- Staff could not tell what Services, Compliance, Identity checks, the Service catalogue and Listings are for. The owner read "Compliance" as "Complaints". (OBSERVED, owner brief)

## Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | **Staff feed.** Ten `staff.*` events, each in one category: REQUEST (new flight or cargo request, customer message, quote accepted in the app); SERVICE (new service request, customer message); SUPPORT (new ticket, customer reply); PAYMENT (invoice paid); IDENTITY (passport sent for checking). New outbox events `support.customer_replied` and `identity.submitted` close the gaps. A new queue, `notify.staff`, consumes them through `StaffFeedService`. Recipients are every active staff member holding the area's read permission, on duty or not. Each gets an INBOX row through the existing `NotificationsService.notify`: one row per person, with read state, under the new audience `STAFF` (default INBOX only, editable in the matrix). What staff did themselves is not news and is skipped. The existing on-duty push/SMS/email alerts are unchanged. |
| D2 | **Read state and live updates.** Three endpoints under `/v1/admin/notifications`: list, summary and read. Read takes exactly one of ids, all, category or ref. The caller's own rows only, staff only. The `/v1/admin/events` stream sends `notifications` to the recipients named in the signal and now also sends `support-changed`. Sidebar badges show this person's unread count per area. Queue sizes stay on the queue tabs. Opening an item (request, cargo, service, ticket, invoice, passport check) marks its notifications read. A toast appears when the unread count grows, whether the stream or the 30 s polling fallback noticed. |
| D3 | **Club points.** An insert-only `loyalty_points_ledger` (GRANTED with a positive delta, DEDUCTED with a negative one, CHECK on the sign, trigger forbids UPDATE and DELETE). Staff with `loyalty.manage` grant or deduct with an internal reason and an optional note for the customer. Rules live in `pointsDelta` (domain): grants only to ACTIVE members; deductions never take the balance below zero and are allowed for SUSPENDED members (corrections); REMOVED members get neither. Deductions are serialised by the member row lock. `GET /v1/me/club` gains `points {balance, history}` without the reason. There is no earning rule: when points are earned and what they are worth are business decisions not taken (UNKNOWN). |
| D4 | **Stripe Invoicing as the payment instrument.** Our invoice stays the record: number, state machine, frozen amounts. For an ISSUED invoice where online payment is offered (flag, ONLINE_PAYMENT policy, expiry, provider; no bypass), the server makes the Stripe calls itself:<br>1. Ensures a Stripe Customer (`stripe_customers`, per user, account and mode, verified email only).<br>2. Creates a `send_invoice` draft with our number, due date = expiry, `auto_advance: false`.<br>3. Adds one invoice item per line (DISCOUNT negative).<br>4. Finalizes it, then checks `amount_due` and currency against our total. A mismatch voids the Stripe invoice and records FAILED.<br><br>Every call carries an idempotency key. Rows live in `stripe_invoices`, one live attempt per invoice. **Automatic for DIRECT invoices** on `invoice.issued` (queue `payments.stripe`). Quote-based invoices keep Embedded Checkout (ADR-0007) unless staff press "Create Stripe invoice". When a Stripe invoice is open, the customer's Pay button opens its hosted page and Checkout is refused, so one invoice can never be paid twice. Webhooks `invoice.finalized`, `paid`, `payment_failed`, `voided`, `marked_uncollectible` and `updated` update the mirror. `invoice.paid` reads the PaymentIntent from the invoice's payments (API 2026-09-30.endive has no `invoice.payment_intent`). It settles through `PaymentsService.recordOnlinePayment`, the code Checkout uses: mismatch check and case, payer screening, payment record, `markPaid`. Cancel or expiry voids the open Stripe invoice. A 10-minute sync catches missed webhooks, voids invoices that are no longer ISSUED, and creates the Stripe invoice for direct invoices whose issue-time job ran inside the 15 s policy cache. Stripe sends no emails of its own: ours carries the link. Live keys stay refused. |
| D5 | **Section guidance.** A "How this works" panel (open until closed once, per browser) on Services, Compliance, Identity checks, the Service catalogue and Listings, plus a help line on the club. The Identity check detail page is now in English and Persian. Nothing was renamed or removed. |
| D6 | **Dashboard.** The Apify usage card is gone from both homes (owner). Spend control stays on the Flight catalog page ("Spend and pacer"), and the Developer Panel Apify page keeps its cap and connection test. The pacer and `/v1/admin/catalog/usage` are unchanged. The On duty control is one line, the height of the queue button, with a status dot. The duplicate `home.onduty/offduty` keys are removed. A Notifications tile was added. |

## Consequences
- Migration 0029 (journal `when` 1793183300000, after 0031, which was already applied locally): `loyalty_points_ledger`, `stripe_customers`, `stripe_invoices`, and INBOX templates for `staff.*`. No new permission, so no Valkey `perm:*` flush is needed.
- Add the six `invoice.*` events to the Stripe webhook endpoint; the Developer Panel checklist lists them.
- Staff with many areas get many rows. Volume is bounded by customer activity. A retention job for old read rows is not built (INFERRED: not needed at current volume).
- Points have no cash value, no expiry and no earning rule until the owner decides them.
- Mobile shows points through `/v1/me/club`. The Flutter screen belongs to the customer-portal work (ADR-0040).
