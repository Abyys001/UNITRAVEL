# ADR-0042 · Cargo operations: tracking numbers, labels, drivers, public tracking, milestone emails

Status: accepted, amended by ADR-0043 (drivers are now **agents**; the role, permissions, tables and screens below were renamed). Owner brief of 19 Nov 2026, "Cargo Management, Shipping Labels & Live Tracking", for admin.unitravels.co.uk and the customer site). Extends Stage 5 (ch. 14), Phase E (STAGE-9) and ADR-0003 (C-rows). Narrows ADR-0025 for cargo tracking numbers only. Stage record: `docs/stages/STAGE-33.md`.

## Context

The cargo module already covers the request form, goods and party screening, quotes, partner booking with AWB, the append-only tracking log, corrections, the estimate and the customer Shipments page. The brief adds the last mile: a tracking number customers can use without signing in, printable labels with QR code and barcode, couriers/drivers with their own restricted panel, proof of collection and delivery, failed attempts, one email per customer-visible milestone, and an operations view in the Admin Panel.

The brief's thirteen statuses are a customer-facing vocabulary. The request state machine (ch. 14.5) stays the single authority for state (rule 4); the new statuses are derived from it and from the tracking log, never stored as a free-form field.

## Decision

1. **Mapping onto the existing model (no parallel module).** "Cargo request" and "cargo shipment" are both `cargo_requests` (one request is one shipment, 1:1, so a second table would only duplicate it); the shipment's operational fields are added to it: `tracking_number`, `preferred_delivery_date`, `handling_instructions`. "Tracking events" are `tracking_events` (append-only, unchanged rules). New tables: `cargo_drivers`, `cargo_driver_assignments`, `cargo_delivery_proofs`, `cargo_labels`, `cargo_notifications`.
2. **Tracking number.** `UT` + 11 digits: 10 random digits and a Luhn check digit (`packages/domain/src/cargo-ops.ts`). Issued once, automatically when the request reaches ACCEPTED (the request is confirmed), or by staff for older shipments. Unique; never reused. The check digit lets the API reject mistyped and invented numbers before any database read.
3. **Customer milestone (derived).** `cargoMilestone(status, exceptionReason, events)` (pure, tested) gives one of REQUEST_RECEIVED, REQUEST_CONFIRMED, AWAITING_COLLECTION, DRIVER_ASSIGNED, COLLECTION_SCHEDULED, COLLECTION_FAILED, CARGO_COLLECTED, IN_TRANSIT, AT_SORTING_FACILITY, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERY_FAILED, DELIVERED, RETURNED_TO_SENDER, ON_HOLD, CANCELLED. New tracking codes feed it: DRIVER_ASSIGNED, COLLECTION_SCHEDULED, COLLECTION_FAILED, SORTING_FACILITY, DELIVERY_FAILED (all staff/driver entries through the existing ingest path, with the same stage gate as Phase E).
4. **Public tracking (narrows ADR-0025).** `GET /v1/public/cargo-tracking/:number` is public, rate-limited per IP (30 per 10 minutes) and answers the same 404 for a wrong check digit and an unknown number. It returns only: the tracking number, service, milestone and label, origin and destination **city and country**, the effective timeline (event label, location as entered, time; no descriptions, authors, sources or correction reasons), the estimate, and on delivery the date and the receiver's initials. Never names, phones, emails, street addresses, notes, prices or documents. ADR-0025 still governs flight-purchase tracking. Staff and drivers enter event locations as a town or facility, not an address (the forms say so).
5. **QR validation.** The label QR opens `<web>/<locale>/tracking/<number>?s=<sig>`, where `sig` is a truncated HMAC-SHA256 of the number keyed from `MASTER_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (domain-separated). The page shows "label verified" when the signature matches; a missing or wrong signature still shows the public view (a customer may type the number), so the signature only proves the label came from us.
6. **Labels.** PDF generated server-side with pdf-lib (already in the stack): one label per piece ("1 of N"), formats `4x6` (thermal 101.6 × 152.4 mm), `A6` and `A4` (two A6 labels per page with cut marks). Each carries the logo wordmark, tracking number, Code128 barcode of the number, the QR code, sender, recipient, collection and delivery address, description, piece weight and dimensions, service, handling instructions. Barcode and QR images come from `bwip-js` 4.11.4 (MIT, pure JS, no native build; approved addition) rendered as PNG and embedded at print resolution. Labels are stored as INTERNAL documents and listed in `cargo_labels`; regenerating voids the previous label row (audited). Labels need a tracking number.
7. **Drivers.** A built-in, non-privileged role `driver` with the single permission `cargo_job.driver`, which also counts as a staff surface so drivers sign in on the Admin Panel host with the normal staff rules (email code + TOTP/passkey second factor, idle timeout). Staff with the new permission `cargo_driver.manage` (admin, cargo_admin, owner) create driver profiles: the profile is linked to an account by email (created when absent, never a customer account takeover: an existing account only gains the `driver` role), and deactivation removes the role and revokes sessions. Granting is bounded to the `driver` role, audited, and bumps `perm_version`.
8. **Assignments and driver authority.** `cargo_driver_assignments` (kind COLLECTION or DELIVERY, one active per kind per request). Every driver route loads the driver's own assignment first and answers 404 otherwise (rule 4). Drivers act through the same state machine: `out_for_delivery` and `deliver` now accept `cargo_job.driver` as well as `cargo_request.transition`, guarded by the fact `assignedDriver` (true only when the actor holds the active DELIVERY assignment). Collection is a PICKED_UP tracking event (allowed from BOOKED, as in Phase E); the hub's `receive` with weighing stays staff-only. Failed collection and failed delivery are tracking events with a reason code; three failed delivery attempts flag the shipment for staff (they decide return to sender through `raise_exception` RETURNED, staff-only).
9. **Proof.** `cargo_delivery_proofs`: receiver name, signature (PNG drawn on the device), photos (JPG/PNG), note, place and time, captured by. Files go through the existing upload pipeline (presigned PUT, ClamAV, EXIF stripped) as INTERNAL documents attached to the request. Delivery needs a receiver name (existing guard) and a signature or a photo.
10. **Milestone emails.** Customer notices for cargo now follow the milestone: after every transition, tracking event, assignment and tracking-number issue the router computes the milestone and inserts `(request, key)` into `cargo_notifications` (unique); only a new row sends. Events: `cargo.milestone` (inbox, push, email) and `cargo.milestone_important` (also SMS: collection scheduled, delivered, failed delivery, returned, on hold, cancelled; same SMS set as `CARGO_SMS_STATUSES`). With no verified email the notice goes by SMS, as for documents (ADR-0006 C8). Variables: customer name, reference, tracking number, status, time (localised), tracking link. AWAITING_COLLECTION is shown but not emailed (it follows confirmation within minutes). `cargo.submitted` to the customer becomes the REQUEST_RECEIVED milestone (inbox + email, SMS when no email). Info requests, declines, quotations and messages keep their own notices. The Admin Panel lists the log with each delivery's status.
10a. **Recipient notices** (added 19 Nov 2026 on the owner's "do all"). When the sender gave the recipient's email, the recipient gets a bilingual email (English and Persian) for OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERY_FAILED and DELIVERED only: the status, the tracking number and the public tracking link, nothing about the sender, contents or price. Sent directly by the email channel (the recipient has no account, inbox or preferences), deduplicated by `cargo_notifications` (`audience = RECIPIENT`, key `RECIPIENT:<milestone key>`), with the send result logged on the row (migration 0033). A failed send removes the row so the job retries.
11. **Admin edits.** Staff may edit addresses, phones, emails, dates and handling instructions of a non-terminal request (audited before/after). Party names, companies, goods and pieces are not editable after screening: changing them would bypass screening (rule 5); staff cancel and ask for a new request instead.
12. **Customer form.** Adds optional preferred delivery date (not before the collection date) and special handling instructions. Collection address is the sender's address, delivery address the recipient's.

13. **Staff intake** (owner request, 19 Nov 2026: "the admin could create cargo also"). Staff with the new non-privileged permission `cargo_request.create.any` (admin, cargo_admin, owner) create a request for a customer from the Admin Panel (`POST /v1/admin/cargo/requests`). The customer is found by an exact email, phone or `UT-C` reference (`GET /v1/admin/cargo/customers?q=`, rate-limited and audited; no browsing), or a new customer account is created from a name and email (unverified until they sign in; customer role only). The request goes through the same contract, lane checks, goods/party/lane screening, state machine and notices as a customer's. **Deviation from ch. 14.3:** the shipper's phone cannot be OTP-verified when staff type it, so for staff intake the phone is stored as entered and the request records `created_by_staff_id` and `intake_channel` (PHONE, IN_PERSON, EMAIL, OTHER); staff must tick that the customer confirmed the declarations, and the submission is audited as `cargo_request.submitted_by_staff`. Notices reach a new customer only once they sign in and verify their email (until then staff hand over the tracking number and the label).

## Consequences

- Anyone holding a tracking number sees the milestone, the two cities and the timeline. That is the purpose of a tracking number; it is random (10^10 space), check-digited and rate-limited.
- Drivers are staff accounts and need a second factor; a driver without a phone that runs an authenticator uses a passkey.
- Mobile (Flutter) is paused (ADR-0026) and not changed.
- New permissions: `cargo_job.driver`, `cargo_driver.manage`. Clear Valkey `perm:*` after deploy.
