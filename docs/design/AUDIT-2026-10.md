# UX and design audit, October 2026 (Phase F)

Scope: owner decision 3, audit and improve the existing "Night flight" system, no new direction. Method: every major screen was
rendered from recorded API data (fixed clock, reduced motion) in en and fa at desktop (web 1360, console 1440) and phone (Pixel 7,
412) widths, checked with axe (WCAG 2.2 A/AA, serious and critical must be zero) and looked at by eye (screenshots in the
`*-snapshots` folders next to `visual.spec.ts`). Flutter: golden images at 390x844 and 1024x768 for five screens. Evidence labels:
OBSERVED (seen in a run), INFERRED (follows from code or a screenshot, not separately tested), UNKNOWN.

## Coverage
Web (19 screens x en/fa x desktop/phone = 76 snapshots + axe): home, sign-in, search results, itinerary, request form, requests list,
request detail, cargo form, cargo detail, account, my details, shipments, billing, invoice, pay (ready, unavailable, paid), pay return
(failed), inbox. Console (18 screens x en/fa x desktop/phone = 72): dashboard, queues, request, quote, cargo, invoices,
verifications (+ detail), compliance (+ case, policies), staff search, customer, Developer Panel home, payments, health, users, audit.
Flutter goldens (24): sign-in, invoice issued, invoice paid, request detail, shipments, pattern gallery, each en/fa x phone/tablet.
Not covered: dark theme snapshots, Flutter admin-mode screens (covered by widget tests), real screen reader pass (UNKNOWN).

## Findings
| # | Finding | Label | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orange-700 text on the warning tint was 4.36:1 (console dashboard, status tags); axe `color-contrast` | OBSERVED | High (AA) | Fixed: token `#a84d00` (5.1:1), web, console and Flutter via the token source |
| 2 | Quote card on request detail: a `<dl>` held a non-`dt/dd` row; axe `definition-list` (en and fa) | OBSERVED | Medium | Fixed: the price line moved out of the list |
| 3 | Developer health dots had `aria-label` on a role-less span; axe `aria-prohibited-attr` | OBSERVED | Medium | Fixed: `role="img"` |
| 4 | Scrollable tables and action rows on the console were not keyboard reachable; axe `scrollable-region-focusable` | OBSERVED | Medium | Fixed: `tabIndex=0` on the scroll containers (ui `Table`, `TableScroll`, action groups) |
| 5 | Case tabs pointed `aria-controls` at a pane that does not exist on the verification page; axe `aria-valid-attr-value` | OBSERVED | Medium | Fixed: attribute dropped (panes still labelled by tab) |
| 6 | 60 native selects in three hand-copied styles; 30 px high on touch screens; native chevron not mirrored | OBSERVED | Medium | Fixed: one `NativeSelect` (44 px on coarse pointers, mirrored chevron). Kept native on purpose, see DESIGN.md |
| 7 | Two web timelines (status, tracking) and two Flutter ones (kit `TimelineList`, tracking `_Stop`) with the same look | OBSERVED | Low | Fixed: one `Timeline` / `AppTimeline` |
| 8 | Payment states were ad-hoc paragraphs; a failed payment used the warning tone | OBSERVED | Medium | Fixed: `PaymentState` (danger for failed, info for processing) and `PaymentStateBanner` |

## What the snapshots do not prove
Snapshots pin appearance for the recorded data, not behaviour; the existing functional specs cover behaviour. Recorded data was captured
from the local stack on 2026-10-24 with the FAKE supplier and payment adapters; nothing in it is real customer data.
